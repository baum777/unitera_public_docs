# A customer request from A to Z

**Disclosure:** PUBLIC_CORE · PUBLIC_ABSTRACTED

This example shows how UNITERA structures realistic work: an employee wants to
prepare a reliable response to a customer request. The flow is conceptual and
does not claim that a live delivery channel is available today.

## Starting point

A customer asks about a service, a date and the next steps. The response should
match current organizational knowledge, make uncertainty visible and create
external effect only after appropriate review.

## 1. KNOW — understand the right context

UNITERA brings together only the context relevant to the request: the customer
question, approved organizational knowledge and traceable sources. Missing or
conflicting information remains visible.

**Result:** an understandable foundation for work instead of an uncontrolled
collection of data.

## 2. THINK — prepare a proposal

A model can structure the information, identify follow-up questions and propose
a response draft. The draft remains a proposal. Good wording or stronger model
performance does not make it automatically correct or binding.

**Result:** a reviewable draft with visible assumptions and open questions.

## 3. Govern — the human decides

The employee reviews facts, tone, purpose and possible effects. If another
approval is required, the responsible decision becomes visible. Without a
sufficient basis or current permission, the work stops or returns for
clarification.

**Result:** a deliberate decision, not authority inferred from the model.

## 4. ACT — only authorized effect

Only when the specific action is currently authorized may it create governed
external effect. Scope and destination remain bounded to the reviewed work.
This example explains the principle, not a particular technical delivery path.

**Result:** authorized effect or a traceable stop.

## 5. PROVE — understand the outcome

After execution, evidence records what was attempted. A technical receipt does
not yet prove that the intended business outcome occurred. Delivery, response
or other outcomes are verified separately.

**Result:** execution evidence and a separate assessment of the actual outcome.

## What the user sees

- `/work` keeps the work, sources, next steps and open decisions together.
- Needs You makes required human involvement visible.
- Chat helps clarify and revise within the context of the work.
- Quick Actions offer bounded next steps without creating permission themselves.

The same pattern applies to decision briefs, internal summaries, meeting
follow-up and other traceable knowledge work: understand, propose, decide, act
within bounds and verify the outcome.

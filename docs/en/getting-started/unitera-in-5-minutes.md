# UNITERA in 5 minutes

**Disclosure:** PUBLIC_CORE · PUBLIC_STATUS

UNITERA helps organizations use AI for real work without handing decisions or
responsibility to a model. It connects reviewed organizational context,
AI-assisted proposals, human decisions and governed effect in a traceable work
flow.

## What does that mean at the start of the day?

A typical start to work looks like this conceptually:

1. You open `/work` and see active work, relevant sources, next steps and decisions that need your involvement.
2. The Company Brain provides your organization's approved context: goals, rules and knowledge relevant to the work.
3. Chat or Quick Actions help you clarify questions, structure information and prepare a next step.
4. You review the proposal. A visible or suggested action is not yet a permission.
5. Only a currently authorized action may create governed effect. Execution evidence and outcome verification remain traceable afterward.

## Five steps without the jargon

| Practical question | UNITERA step | Result |
|---|---|---|
| What do I need to know? | **KNOW** | Relevant, traceable context |
| What are the options? | **THINK** | Analysis, draft or proposal |
| May and do I want to do this? | **Govern** | Human or institutional decision |
| What should actually happen? | **ACT** | Only the authorized, bounded effect |
| What actually happened? | **PROVE** | Evidence and separate outcome verification |

## Key product elements

- **Company Brain:** the governed shared context of an organization — not model memory or execution permission.
- **`/work`:** the institutional work surface for active work, sources, next steps and required decisions.
- **Needs You:** shows work requiring human participation or a decision.
- **Chat and Quick Actions:** help people understand, structure and prepare work; they do not create additional rights.
- **Personal Realm and Companion:** preserve personal continuity separately from institutional context.

## Three simple examples

- **Prepare a customer response:** bring sources together, produce a draft, review it and create effect only when permission exists.
- **Prepare a decision brief:** bring the situation, options, uncertainty and open decisions together in a traceable form.
- **Resume work:** see the last reviewed context, open questions and next useful step without silently inheriting earlier rights.

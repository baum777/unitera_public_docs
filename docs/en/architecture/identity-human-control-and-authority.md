# Identity, Human Control and Authority

**Disclosure:** PUBLIC_CORE · PUBLIC_ABSTRACTED · PUBLIC_STATUS

UNITERA separates a person's evidenced identity, their eligibility for a
specific human decision, and permission for the resulting effect.

| Layer | Question | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Which natural person is evidenced? Do two identity references refer to the same person or to different people? | Establishes no tenant membership, role or decision authority. |
| **Human Control** | Does this person satisfy the required human role and, where applicable, independence for this specific decision? | Creates no execution permission by itself. |
| **Authority** | May the intended effect actually occur under current rules and bounded permissions? | Remains separate from identity confirmation and approval. |

## Identity establishes persons, not organizational rights

A successful sign-in first establishes authenticated access. Resolution to a
natural person requires its own current evidence. An account, session or device
alone is insufficient.

Identity can also establish whether two references mean the same natural person
or different natural persons. This person relationship is not an owner
relationship, tenant membership or delegation. Organizational roles and rights
are established separately.

Two different accounts or IDs do not prove two different people. An unresolved
person relationship stays unresolved; missing evidence is not treated as
distinctness.

## Human Control applies to the specific decision

Human participation alone is insufficient evidence. What matters is whether
the specific person satisfies the requirements of the relevant decision type.
These include current identity, the required role and decision authority,
and any required independence.

A confirmed identity therefore does not make someone a universal approver.
An owner role does not answer every decision question either. Where independence
is required, evidenced distinctness of natural persons is a prerequisite; it
does not automatically satisfy the other control requirements. Nor does it
create a universal requirement for a second human.

Where applicable rules require no individual human approval, no artificial
human control step is added. Other permission and effect boundaries still apply.

## Decision and effect remain separate

A human decision concerns a specific proposal under specific rules. If material
decision inputs or applicable rules change, an earlier positive evaluation
cannot simply be reused. Stale, contradictory or revoked evidence requires a
new evaluation or prevents the action.

An approval is not a grant. A grant is a separate, bounded and revocable
permission under defined conditions. Satisfied human control replaces neither
that permission nor current execution checks. Conversely, a grant does not
replace stale or missing human control.

## Owner and Guard perform different functions

A bounded founding profile allows preparation of a new tenant and its first
owner through one human owner decision plus independent Guard assurance.
This profile requires no second natural person.

| Function | Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Makes the explicitly bound human decision. |
| **Guard** | Independently evaluates the same specific act, separate from the proposing cognition path. |
| **Grant** | Bounds the separately permitted effect. |
| **Executor** | Executes within currently permitted boundaries. |

The Guard is not a natural person, a second approver, or grant or execution
authority. It does not replace a person when the relevant decision type requires
independent human control. In the founding profile, the owner cannot override
a blocking Guard finding.

This profile applies only to initial tenant and owner establishment. Later
membership, role or delegation changes use their own requirements. Establishment
creates no capability grant and activates neither pilot nor production.

## Semantics are not a live availability claim

**Scoped source review:** 6 October 2026

The separation is contractually described. Complete implementation and
qualification of the current human control path remains separate work.
Confirmed identity, semantic adoption or source publication provides no blanket
proof of active human control or production autonomy. The
[public maturity projection](../status/current-state.md) remains a separate,
dated assessment.

The short form is: **Identity establishes the person. Human Control establishes
eligibility for this decision. Authority establishes whether the effect is permitted.**

# Governed agentic work

Status: `PUBLIC_ABSTRACTED`

UNITERA is an AI operating system **within which specialized agents and capabilities collaborate**. The operating system and the agents are not competing approaches. People define goals, inspect consequential decisions and can pause or take over work.

## From a request to a reviewable outcome

1. **Understand:** A work item receives purpose-bound, source-aware context. Uncertainty stays visible.
2. **Plan:** Tasks are broken into steps, dependencies and expected evidence. An agent-generated plan is not permission to execute.
3. **Coordinate:** Qualified models, specialized agents, tools and people carry out suitable parts of the work.
4. **Govern:** Executor eligibility, data access and authorization for external effects remain separate. Binding external actions require current permission and human approval where required.
5. **Prove:** Execution, receipt, required independent checking, reconciliation of uncertain effects and completion are distinct steps.

New evidence may change a plan. But revision, delegation and model changes never silently extend the permitted scope of effects. An uncertain external result does not justify a blind retry.

## Purpose and maturity boundary

The goal is not autonomy for its own sake but understandable, accountable collaboration. People should be able to achieve useful results with AI while developing their own judgment, capabilities and ability to act.

Workflow orchestration is locally executable and evidenced in bounded live experiments. This **does not activate a general agent service or production autonomy**. Real external effects, integration, service exposure and sustained operation need separate qualification and authorization.

> Conceptual public projection — not deployment, service, repository, protocol or security topology.

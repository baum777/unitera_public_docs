## What changed since the previous snapshot

Several surfaces that were previously described only as owner-confirmed direction now have verifiable bounded materialization. This includes native work and continuity surfaces, personal resume and contribution preparation, and the versioned bounded pilot and execution grammar.

The claim boundary remains strict:

- materialized UI or runtime does not mean live activation;
- a frozen pilot scope creates neither a grant nor execution permission;
- a client may display or request authority but cannot create it;
- technical receipt or provider evidence is not automatically a verified business outcome;
- real external connectivity, current admission, end-to-end evidence and explicit pilot activation remain separate gates;
- unmerged candidates are not counted as current state.

## Cross-repository reconciliation rule

Current state is derived from the responsible owner sources. Registry, cartography, documentation or other derived projections can lag behind an already-canonical owner state. Such projection lag may neither override the owner state nor artificially downgrade it.

The [capability and use-case matrix](capability-use-case-matrix.md) breaks this classification down by product function, expected result and boundary.

## Does not imply

- `PILOT` does not mean `CONFORMANT`.
- bounded implementation does not mean live availability.
- materialized capability does not mean execution permission.
- public evidence does not by itself establish operational truth beyond its stated scope.

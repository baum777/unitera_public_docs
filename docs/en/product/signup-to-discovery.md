# From sign-in to Discovery

Status: `PUBLIC_CORE`

The public journey describes product behavior, not authentication or service topology.

1. Sign-in establishes authenticated access; the natural person is evidenced separately.
2. Access to organizational context requires separately checked membership and permission. Sign-in alone creates neither.
3. Initial tenant and owner establishment requires its own explicitly authorized founding profile; it is not general self-service permission.
4. Discovery develops reviewable organizational understanding from approved foundations.
5. Review and explicit activation follow their own requirements before the relevant context is actively used in `/work`.

[Identity, Human Control and Authority](../architecture/identity-human-control-and-authority.md) explains the separate prerequisites for person, decision and effect.

Discovery helps capture mission, operating model, boundaries, sources and open questions in a traceable way. Results remain reviewable before they become active institutional context.

> Conceptual public projection — not deployment, service, repository, protocol or security topology.

# Operating surface and continuity

Status: `PUBLIC_CORE`

The core semantics of Work, Needs You, Quick Actions, Chat and Resume are confirmed. Newer surface details on this page represent owner-confirmed product direction whose canonical source adoption is still pending. This page creates neither runtime nor pilot authorization.

UNITERA connects personal continuity and institutional work through a shared interaction grammar without collapsing their meaning or authority boundaries. Native, web and mobile surfaces may use different density and entry points, but they do not create different rights.

## Today and Resume

On mobile surfaces, **Today** is the deliberate restart point. Within the same usage session, an open work item can be resumed directly. A desktop-oriented surface may show Today and Resume together without automatically taking focus.

Opening a work item does not start an effect. A human-set pause persists across device and session changes.

## Focus and human participation

Active work remains stable until the user chooses to switch. New events update overviews but do not silently take focus.

Needs You is primarily a queue for required human participation. Visible urgency must follow from a concrete reason such as a deadline, dependency or impact. Active interruptions remain exceptional and do not automatically switch the work context.

Technical problems are presented as concrete work impact: the affected step, last verified state and next clarification step.

## Quick Actions and Chat

Quick Actions combine stable general entry points with actions that match the current context. Visibility does not mean executability.

Chat and the composer may look similar in personal, work-bound and tenant-wide use. Their semantic context remains explicit. Switching between personal and institutional realms is never silent; a personal contribution crosses the boundary only after explicit confirmation.

## Approvals and action packages

A single interaction may approve an exactly displayed action package. That approval applies only to the displayed revision, targets and sequence; it does not expand durable delegation.

Relevant prerequisites are checked again immediately before an effect. A material change requires a new decision. Partial results and uncertain outcomes remain visible. An uncertain external effect triggers reconciliation rather than a blind retry.

## Trust across surfaces

Freshness, evidence, connectivity and processing location remain compactly visible in the normal state. Relevant problems such as stale data, offline state, conflicts or an uncertain outcome expand the detail shown.

```text
Same surface != same command
Visible action != executable action
Resume != authority
Pause persists until explicitly lifted
Unknown outcome != safe retry
```

The first coherent product acceptance target is an end-to-end, understandable work path that combines personal resume, institutional preparation, required human decision and clear outcome evidence. It does not authorize production.

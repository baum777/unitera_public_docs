# Discovery and Company Brain

Status: `PUBLIC_CORE`

The Company Brain is an organization's versioned institutional context. It is neither model memory, an agent nor execution permission.

Discovery gathers and structures traceable foundations. People review uncertainty, conflicts and open decisions. Only explicit activation makes a reviewed state active institutional context.

In `/work`, this context appears as an understandable foundation for active work, sources, next steps and Needs You — not as a technical object tree.

## Needs You, Quick Actions and Chat

Needs You gathers work that requires a human decision or human participation. The collection is tenant-scoped and access-controlled. Inclusion in Needs You marks required involvement, not urgency; any urgency shown derives from a deadline or concrete impact.

Quick Actions provide concise next steps — generally available ones as well as those matching the currently open work. Whether an action can actually be executed depends on current rights, prerequisites and the applicable permission and approval state. The visibility of an action is not a permission.

Chat supports free-form questions, requests and refinement of ongoing work — contextual to the current work or tenant-wide. `/work` remains the primary institutional operating surface; Chat is a central, complementary surface, not an unimportant option.

Neither Chat nor Quick Actions create their own decision, approval or execution authority. Binding effects remain subject to the same approval and permission rules regardless of the entry point.

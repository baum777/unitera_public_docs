# Personal Realm and Companion

Status: `PUBLIC_CORE`

The Personal Realm is the personal continuity domain. The Companion helps people resume, remember, continue thinking and prepare a possible contribution to institutional work.

## Experience

- **Resume:** see the last state, sources, open questions and next useful step.
- **Remember:** maintain traceable personal memory with correction and forgetting controls.
- **Circling:** keep thoughts visible before they become committed work.
- **Context continuation:** carry bounded context into a new session, device or model.
- **Prepare a contribution:** prepare personal ideas as a draft; explicit confirmation is required before institutional handoff.

```text
Personal Memory != Company Brain
Familiarity != Permission
Resume context != Authority
Prepared contribution != accepted institutional claim
```

Local and cross-device continuity does not expand rights. Personal and institutional data remain separated by an explicit boundary.

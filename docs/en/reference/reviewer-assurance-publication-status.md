# Review and assurance

Status: `PUBLIC_ABSTRACTED`

UNITERA separates domain review, technical verification and authority. Reviewers may assess evidence and surface risk, but review does not create execution permission.

Only coarse maturity is published. Specific reviewer assignments, internal classes, test states and bindings remain in the responsible internal sources.

Independent Guard assurance evaluates the specific act but remains separate
from the human decision role. A Guard is not a human approver and fills no
required second human control slot. Conversely, evidenced distinctness of
natural persons alone does not prove complete satisfaction of human control
requirements.

Repository publication review, human decision, Guard assurance and outcome
verification establish different facts. None of these checks alone creates a
grant. The [Identity / Human Control semantics](../architecture/identity-human-control-and-authority.md) explain the boundaries.

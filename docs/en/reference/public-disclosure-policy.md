# Public disclosure policy

UNITERA publishes a curated, deliberately lossy projection of its principles, product behavior and bounded architectural concepts.

```text
Public documentation explains WHAT and WHY.
Internal owner sources define HOW, WHERE and UNDER WHICH CONTRACT.
```

## Classes

- **PUBLIC_CORE:** Human Agency, Company Brain, Personal Realm, `/work`, Needs You, Chat, Quick Actions, KNOW/THINK/ACT, governed execution, evidence, verification and continuity.
- **PUBLIC_ABSTRACTED:** authority separation, organizational boundaries, local runtime, model selection, execution control, memory, provenance, portability, review and recovery — only without reconstructable implementation topology.
- **PUBLIC_STATUS:** coarse maturity only: concept, contracted, under implementation, pilot preparation, limited pilot or production.
- **RESTRICTED_SOURCE_DETAIL:** exact repositories and refs, internal IDs, object/schema/package names, providers, credentials, protocols, routes, state enums, enforcement order, and test/CI topology.

## Composition rule

Two individually acceptable facts are not published together when their combination would reconstruct internal ownership, authority, runtime or enforcement structures.

Exact sources are verified internally but are not automatically published. Transparency means explainable principles and boundaries, not operational reconstructability.

Engineering state changes continuously. Public architectural state changes deliberately when canonical architecture, product commitments or public maturity change — not for every merge or internal gate.
